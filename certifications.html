Market Leadership

Certified for Quality. Approved for Scale.

Every BPP structure is backed by third-party quality management certification, structural validation from IIT Kanpur, and long-standing registrations with India’s most demanding government and institutional buyers.

Quality & Structural Certifications

Marks of Assurance

ISO 9001:2015

Quality management systems. Certifies that BPP’s manufacturing processes, from raw material intake to final dispatch, follow a documented, consistently audited quality system.

ISO 14001:2015

Environmental management systems. Governs how the Baidyabati plant manages material use, effluents and environmental impact through its galvanizing and fabrication processes.

ISO 45001:2018

Occupational health & safety management. Certifies workplace safety systems across heavy fabrication, crane handling and hot-dip galvanizing operations.

Wind Velocity Certification

Issued by IIT Kanpur. Structural validation confirming that BPP mast designs withstand site-specific wind loading, from coastal cyclonic zones to high-altitude terrain.

JAS-ANZ

Joint Accreditation System of Australia and New Zealand. An internationally recognised accreditation forum that validates the certification bodies auditing BPP’s quality systems.

BPP POLE / PCMS

Perfection Commitment Certified Process. BPP’s internal quality assurance protocol governing dimensional accuracy, galvanizing thickness and finish on every pole and mast produced.

IR Class Certification

Qualifies BPP as an approved manufacturer of railway masts and structures to Indian Railways’ class standards.

Design & Validation

Technology Validated By

BPP’s manufacturing process is completely indigenous, built on technology derived from extensive research by a team of specialised engineers since 1994. Designs have been appraised and approved by IIT Kharagpur and Jadavpur University; wind velocity certification is held from IIT Kanpur. An in-house design and drawing facility enables continuous R&D and site-specific engineering.
