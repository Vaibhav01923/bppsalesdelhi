Manufacturing Strength

Galvanized In-House, In a Single Dip

Long sections galvanized in one piece have no swaged joint and no internal cavity in which pickling acid can be trapped. That removes the single most common cause of premature failure in conventional welded and swaged poles — corrosion working outward from inside the joint.
