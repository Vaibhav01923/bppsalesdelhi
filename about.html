For three decades, B.P. Projects has stood at the intersection of engineering precision and national infrastructure ambition. What began in 1994 has grown into one of India’s most trusted names in high mast and steel lighting infrastructure — a journey I am privileged to carry forward, building on the shared vision of our late Chairman, Mr. Bhola Jaiswal, and myself.

Who We Are

Three Decades of Indigenous Engineering

B.P. Projects Pvt. Ltd. (BPP), headquartered in Kolkata, has pioneered hot-dip galvanized steel lighting infrastructure in India since 1994 — building a reputation for engineering excellence, financial strength and dependable delivery, with turnover exceeding ₹75 crore in FY 2024–25.

Our manufacturing process is entirely indigenous, built on technology validated by IIT Kanpur, IIT Delhi, IIT Kharagpur and Jadavpur University. BPP holds a Wind Velocity Certification from IIT Kanpur, engineering every structure to withstand extreme environmental stress — a standard few Indian manufacturers can claim.

From standard poles of 3 metres to monumental high masts exceeding 70 metres, our range spans High Masts, Stadium Masts, Flag Masts, Signage Masts and uniformly tapered Smart Conical and Octagonal poles — all manufactured to IS standards and finished entirely in-house at our own galvanizing plant.

BPP holds long-standing centralized registrations with CPWD and Indian Railways, alongside multi-state approvals from NHAI, Delhi PWD, MCD, DDA, UP PWD, Haryana PWD and Gujarat PWD — credentials built over three decades of consistent, on-time delivery.

Why Single-Dip Matters

Long sections galvanized in one piece have no swaged joint and no internal cavity in which pickling acid can be trapped. That removes the single most common cause of premature failure in conventional welded and swaged poles — corrosion working outward from inside the joint.
